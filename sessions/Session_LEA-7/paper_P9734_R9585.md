# Dynamic monitoring of non-agriculturalization of cultivated land based on SNUNet3+ model——A case study of Northern Jiangsu Province

**Author Information**  
Lizhi Miao<sup>1</sup>, Kaiwen Wu<sup>1</sup>, Xinting Li<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications  



## Abstract

Rapid urbanization in China conflicts with cultivated land preservation, necessitating advanced monitoring to safeguard food security. This study deploys the SNUNet3+ deep learning model to monitor the non-agriculturalization of cultivated land in Northern Jiangsu Province (Xuzhou, Lianyungang, Suqian, and Yancheng) using high-resolution GF-2 satellite imagery from 2018 and 2023. To overcome traditional CNN limitations, we utilized the SNUNet3+ architecture, integrating a Siamese encoder with dense skip connections to preserve fine-grained localization. Furthermore, scSE attention mechanism and deep supervision modules were incorporated to enhance detection accuracy for small, irregular targets. Large-scale remote sensing data was processed efficiently using rigorous preprocessing and a sliding window prediction method. The empirical results reveal that 805.85 km^2 of cultivated land (1.77% of the total area) was converted to non-agricultural uses over the five-year period. Xuzhou experienced the extensive conversion (344.17 km^2), primarily driven by urban and industrial expansion. Validating these results against official statistical data demonstrated high consistency, yielding a total relative error of only 2.98% across the four cities.

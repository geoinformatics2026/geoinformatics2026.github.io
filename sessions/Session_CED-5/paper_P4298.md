# Bridging the Modality Gap in Optical-SAR Change Detection via Frequency-Aware and Cross-Modal Difference Attention

**Author Information**  
Yang Yang<sup>1</sup>, Jiangong Xu<sup>1</sup>, Jun Pan<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> State Key Laboratory of Information Engineering in Surveying, Mapping and Remote Sensing, Wuhan University  



## Abstract

Optical and synthetic aperture radar (SAR) heterogeneous image change detection (HCD) plays a crucial role in disaster emergency response and assessment. However, due to significant differences in imaging mechanisms, precisely extracting cross-modal change information remains an exceptionally challenging task. Existing methods often struggle to effectively align cross-modal features. To address this limitation, we propose a novel framework, the Optical- SAR Change Detection Network (OSCDNet), that integrates a dual-branch frequency attention mechanism and a Cross- Modal Difference Attention (CMDA) module to precisely delineate changed regions. Experimental results demonstrate that OSCDNet effectively bridges the cross-modal semantic gap and significantly outperforms most existing change detection methods.



Keywords: Heterogeneous change detection, Optical-SAR images, Cross-modal attention, Frequency attention, Feature alignment

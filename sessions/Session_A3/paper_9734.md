## Abstract

Rapid urbanization in China conflicts with cultivated land preservation, necessitating advanced monitoring to safeguard food security. This study deploys the SNUNet3+ deep learning model to monitor the non-agriculturalization of cultivated land in Northern Jiangsu Province (Xuzhou, Lianyungang, Suqian, and Yancheng) using high-resolution GF-2 satellite imagery from 2018 and 2023. To overcome traditional CNN limitations, we utilized the SNUNet3+ architecture, integrating a Siamese encoder with dense skip connections to preserve fine-grained localization. Furthermore, scSE attention mechanism and deep supervision modules were incorporated to enhance detection accuracy for small, irregular targets. Large-scale remote sensing data was processed efficiently using rigorous preprocessing and a sliding window prediction method. Validating these results against official statistical data demonstrated high consistency, yielding a total relative error of only 2.98% across the four cities.

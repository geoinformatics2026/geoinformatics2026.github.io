# Research on the Cloud Computing-Based Technical Architecture for Meteorological and Oceanographic Services

**Author Information**  
Na Li<sup>1</sup>, Yanwen Wang<sup>1</sup>, Qi Lv<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> National University of Defense Technology  



## Abstract

This study designs an advanced technical architecture for Meteorological and Oceanographic (METOC) service systems to address the challenges of big data and real-time processing in the information age. By integrating core cloud computing features such as on-demand self-service, resource pooling, and rapid elasticity, a multi-tier cloud-native platform is constructed. This platform encompasses infrastructure, data processing, and application service layers, and is implemented through a hybrid deployment model alongside a user-centric service delivery framework. The proposed architecture aims to significantly enhance operational capabilities in critical areas including multi-source data fusion, high-resolution numerical modeling, and timely product dissemination within the METOC field. Preliminary prototyping and testing have verified its feasibility and effectiveness in improving system agility, resource utilization, and operational efficiency. The work provides a practical technical solution and a valuable reference for building a next-generation, flexible, collaborative, and cost-effective METOC cloud service system.



Keywords: Meteorological and Oceanographic service systems,  Cloud Computing-Based Technical Architecture,  service system

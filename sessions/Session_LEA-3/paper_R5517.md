# Spatial evolution of urban-rural fringe and its influences on ecological land fragmentation: a case study of Suzhou City, China

**Author Information**  
Yasi Tian<sup>1</sup>, Yice Zhang<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> soochow university  



## Abstract

Urban-rural fringe (URF) areas are characterized by dynamic land-use changes and complex ecological pressures. Based on exploring the key factors influencing ecological land fragmentation in different URF evolution regions to formulate targeted planning and governance measures, this study takes Suzhou, which is a mega-city in China, as a case study. It analyzes the spatial evolution of URF and investigates the factors influencing ecological land fragmentation across various URF regions. First, the URF of Suzhou in 2005 and 2022 were identified based on a deep neural network method and six URF evolution regions were identified. Second, using multi-dimensional landscape metrics, ecological land fragmentation was measured based on a spatial principal component analysis. Finally, influencing factors of ecological land fragmentation in different URF evolution regions were explored based on a random forest method. It concluded the followings: (1) From 2005 to 2022, the URF in Suzhou grew from 1133 km² to 2481 km², and the evolution of URF is featured by rural-to-fringe and fringe-to-urban transformations. (2) Ecological land fragmentation varies spatially across the URF, with rural-to-fringe and fringe-to-urban regions experiencing the highest increases. (3) Factors influencing fragmentation differ across regions: construction land expansion drives changes in rural-to-fringe and fringe-to-urban areas, while human activity intensity, such as nightlight and POI density, shapes fragmentation in consistently fringe and fringe-to-urban regions. This study highlights the necessity and importance of identifying URF and distinguishing the different influencing factors of ecological land fragmentation in different URF evolution regions to enhance ecological connectivity and support sustainable urban-rural transitions.

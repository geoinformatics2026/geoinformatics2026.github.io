# The collaborative optimization of existing and additional construction land in urban areas considering land use efficiency

**Author Information**  
Chun Li<sup>1</sup>, Shumeng Du<sup>1</sup>, Shanshan Chen<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Yunnan University  



## Abstract

Upon reaching a stable phase of urbanization, the development of urban areas no longer solely relies on urban expansion but instead begins to integrate the renewal of existing urban land. However, the collaborative optimization of existing and additional urban land still requires in-depth exploration. We focus on land use efficiency to explore such collaborative optimization. Utilizing geographic big data, we conduct efficiency evaluation and potential analysis of both existing and additional urban land. Considering future land demand, multiple land use scenarios are developed based on diverse optimization strategies of land use efficiency. Central Yunnan Urban Agglomeration area (CYUA) is taken as a case study. The results indicate that, in CYUA, a positive spatial correlation between land use efficiency and land demand, and a negative spatial correlation between potential for optimizing land use efficiency. Under the scenario dominated by existing land optimization, the existing construction land in CYUA could be reduced by approximately 66.97%. In the additional land supplement scenario, an extra 111.84 km² of construction land is needed, accounting for about 11.91% of the current construction land. In the scenario of collaborative optimization of existing and additional land, a 15% improvement in existing land use efficiency could eliminate the need for additional land. The analytical framework established in this study can provide technical support for exploring collaborative development pathways for future existing and additional urban land, and serve as a foundational reference for resolving urban land conflicts.



Keywords: urban expansion,  urban renewal,  land use efficiency,  urban agglomeration

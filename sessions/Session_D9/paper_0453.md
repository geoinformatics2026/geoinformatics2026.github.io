# Research on the Reform of Surveying and Mapping Professional Talent Cultivation Model Based on 'Master Craftsmen - Curriculum - Evaluation'

## Abstract

Guided by the Digital China strategy, surveying and mapping geoinformation, serving as the spatiotemporal foundation, is vigorously propelling the digital transformation of the cultural and tourism industry. In the face of new trends such as cross-border integration, interdisciplinary convergence, and upgrading of job competencies during this transformation, the industry urgently needs to cultivate compound technical and skilled personnel with cross-professional integration capabilities and the ability to perform multiple roles. This paper, relying on the in-depth college-enterprise collaboration and effective implementation in surveying and mapping geoinformation majors, addresses three core issues commonly existing in talent cultivation: insufficient collaboration between master craftsmen, lack of systematic curriculum design, and inadequate incentive and guiding functions of teaching evaluation. Based on the "synergetics" theory, it systematically designs the master craftsmen team, curriculum system, and teaching evaluation, and establishes an advanced technical and skilled talent cultivation model of "master craftsmen collaboration, competition-research- application-innovation, and evaluation empowerment, " achieving remarkable results.



Keywords: Surveying and mapping,  Master craftsmen,  Cultivation model,  Curriculum system,  Evaluation

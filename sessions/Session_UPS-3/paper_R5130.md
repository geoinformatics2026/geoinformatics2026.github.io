# Seeing the Street from Space: Cross-View Human Perception Learning via AlphaEarth

**Author Information**  
Peilin Li<sup>1</sup>, Pengfei Chen<sup>1</sup>, Xiao Cheng<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Sun Yat-sen University  



## Abstract

Street-level human perceptions, such as safety and pleasantness, significantly impact urban quality of life but remain difficult to measure at scale due to the high costs and uneven coverage of traditional street-view imagery. This study proposes an innovative cross-view deep learning framework to predict street-level perceptions without relying solely on street-view data. We utilize a dual-branch fusion MLP model that integrates high-dimensional remote sensing embeddings from Google AlphaEarth with multi-source urban contextual data. Tested across four major Southeast Asian cities (Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, and Manila), the fusion model achieves a robust R² of 0.7–0.9 at the road-segment aggregation level, significantly outperforming traditional machine learning methods. Leveraging the trained models, we generated high-resolution perception maps for full urban road networks and conducted a population exposure equity analysis using WorldPop data and Gini coefficients. This research demonstrates a scalable, cost-effective methodology for urban perception mapping in data-scarce environments, providing valuable new tools for urban planning and social equity research.



Keywords: Street perception prediction,  Cross-view deep learning,  Population exposure equity

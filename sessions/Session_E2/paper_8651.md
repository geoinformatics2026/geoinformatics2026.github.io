# Contrasting Drivers of Water Clarity: A Multi-Decadal Satellite Analysis of Two Types of Lake Wetlands

**Author Information**  
Yelong Zhao<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Northeast Institute of Geography and Agroecology Chinese Academy of Sciences  



## Abstract

Wetlands are unique ecological systems that integrates both aquatic and terrestrial landscape characteristics. However, water quality is threatened by environmental and anthropogenic factors. Therefore, analyzing the variations of water clarity (Secchi disk depth, Zsd) across various types of lake wetlands and revealing related driving factors are crucial for effective monitoring and assessment. The study utilized Landsat series data to analyze the spatiotemporal dynamics of Zsd and its association with driving factors (climatic factors, land use, and landscape) in typical river-flow and non-river-flow lake wetlands since 1984, focusing on four representative wetlands: two river-flow lake wetlands (Dongting Lake and Poyang Lake) and two non-river-flow lake wetlands (Baiyangdian and Hengshui Lake). The findings revealed that Zsd variations and spatial variability in river-flow lake wetlands were higher than those in non-river flow lake wetlands, which may be due to abundant precipitation and more frequent water exchanges. Distinct inter-annual trends pattern in Zsd were observed, characterized by peak values followed by declines. These inter-annual variations were driven not only by climate fluctuations but also variations by land use and landscape. Quantitative analysis of the variations of Zsd in two types of lake wetlands was performed to determine the driving mechanisms and differing responses mechanisms of Zsd to climate, land use, and landscape The research provides an important data foundation and scientific basis for monitoring and management of large-scale lake wetlands under diverse environmental and anthropogenic factors.



Keywords: Landsat,  Lake wetland,  Water clarity,  Land use,  Landscape

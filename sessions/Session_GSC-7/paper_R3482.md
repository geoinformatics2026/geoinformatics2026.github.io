# On the support issue of spatially intensive variables

**Author Information**  
Xiang Ye<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Nanjing Normal University  



## Abstract

Spatially intensive variable is one of the most common types of variables describing geographic phenomena. However, the seemingly unified concept in fact encompasses variables of different natures, with some behaving as expected but others less typical. By demonstrating the different cases of “spatially intensive variables”, this study identifies that the different aspatial supports and the proxy structure of supports are the main causes of the internal inconsistency of spatially intensive variables. Considering this, a new framework of taxonomy of geographic variables shall be initiated to hopefully mitigate the issue and advance spatial data science in the new era.

# Transferring group knowledge from LLMs: The case of urban perception

**Author Information**  
Ce Hou<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology  



## Abstract

Understanding the perceptual differences among various urban groups is crucial. Traditional survey methods are time-consuming, labor-intensive, and have privacy issues, whereas AI methods only provide general results based on the survey data. This study proposes a new framework based on a Large Language Model (LLM) that can zero-shotly adapt general evaluation results to specific groups. The framework first utilizes the LLM’s world knowledge to generate descriptions of safety perceptions for specific populations (by age and gender) in particular scenarios (such as traffic accidents and criminal activities). It then fine-tunes a general model using a pseudolabeling strategy. Experiments in Stockholm confirmed that this method can increase evaluation accuracy for specific groups by 14.9% to 24%. The framework's application in Hong Kong also successfully revealed significant safety perception differences across various districts based on age and gender, providing governments with a universal and practical tool for assessing diverse urban perceptions.



Keywords: Large language model,  safety perception,  street view image,  urban visual intelligence

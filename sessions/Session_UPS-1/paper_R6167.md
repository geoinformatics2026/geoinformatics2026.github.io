# Mapping socioeconomic indicators using multi-source geospatial data

**Author Information**  
Yeran Sun<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Aerospace Information Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences  



## Abstract

The study explores the potential of multi-source data in mapping socioeconomic indicators, providing technical support for urban and rural planning. Using the United Kingdom as a case study, it integrates remote sensing and social sensing data—such as nighttime light remote sensing imagery and social media geotagged data—with socioeconomic statistical data (e.g., census data) to construct spatiotemporal models for mapping socioeconomic indicators. This research enhances the precision of spatiotemporal modeling of socioeconomic indicators and advances the study of human-environment interactions toward a more quantitative, human-centric, and accurate direction. It also offers decision-making support for regional integration development and contributes to the creation of new-type cities that are livable, green, resilient, smart, and culturally enriched.



Keywords: social sensing,  big data,  geocomputation,  social remote sensing,  spatiotemporal modelling

# Automated Generation of Schematic Tourist Maps Using Fine-Tuned LLM and Geospatial Optimization

**Author Information**  
Zhiwei Wu<sup>1</sup>, Donglin Cheng<sup>1</sup>, Chenzhen Sun<sup>1</sup>, Tian Lan<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Southwest Jiaotong University  



## Abstract

A well-designed tourist map can significantly enhance a traveler's experience by offering a more organized and informative journey. Despite this, current digital map service platforms are limited in their ability to automatically generate customized route maps that cater to diverse tourist preferences. To bridge this gap, this paper proposes a novel framework that integrates large language models (LLMs) with schematic representation techniques to enable the automated generation of personalized tourist route maps. In this framework, a tourism-specific LLM is first developed through parameter-efficient fine-tuning using two types of domain-specific corpora, enabling the model to generate accurate and context-aware travel recommendations. Subsequently, a Voronoi-diagram-based algorithm is employed to optimize the spatial distribution of attractions, while a grid-based path optimization algorithm is used to construct schematic routes based on the recommendations provided by the fine-tuned LLM. The proposed method is evaluated through both objective quantitative metrics (e.g., BLEU-4) and subjective user surveys involving more than 300 participants. Experimental results demonstrate that the fine-tuned LLM outperforms baseline models in generating relevant attraction and route suggestions. Moreover, the generated four-direction schematic tourist route maps exhibit significant advantages over conventional maps that retain precise geographic locations, particularly in terms of legibility, ease of recognition of attractions and routes, aesthetic quality, and overall user satisfaction. These findings suggest that the proposed framework is effective in producing high-usability, customized tourist route maps. Overall, this study offers a novel pathway from user demand input to schematic map output, advancing the role of cartography in empowering intelligent tourism services.



Keywords: tourist maps,  tourist demands,  large language models,  schematic representation,  customized mapping

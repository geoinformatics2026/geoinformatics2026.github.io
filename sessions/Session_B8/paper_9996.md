# A method for constructing a three-dimensional geological model based on the alignment of geological maps and text

## Abstract

Three-dimensional geological models (3DGE) are essential for subsurface visualization, regional investigations, and engineering assessment. However, their accuracy is often constrained by the limited availability of borehole data, particularly in data-scarce regions. Constructing reliable 3D geological models under sparse drilling conditions remains a significant technical challenge. This study proposes a robust, knowledge-driven framework for reconstructing complex subsurface structures without relying on physical boreholes, based on a novel “entity-relationship” network. The proposed approach establishes an integrated pipeline for fusing heterogeneous geological information sources. Large Language Models (LLMs), guided by structured prompt constraints, are employed to extract key stratigraphic and chronological information from geological texts, while deep learning techniques are used to infer spatial distributions from geological maps. The extracted knowledge is organized into an extensible network that encodes geological ages, stratigraphic hierarchies, and spatial relationships. The principal innovation of this framework is the generation of virtual boreholes through strategic sampling along inferred stratigraphic sequences, transforming structural attitudes and map-derived geometries into vertical constraints suitable for three-dimensional modeling. This mechanism enables the efficient construction of multi-scale geological models that satisfy engineering accuracy requirements while maintaining computational efficiency. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method effectively integrates textual and cartographic information, achieving a profile consistency rate of 86.71% when compared with reference geological sections. The proposed framework provides an efficient and scalable solution for regional 3D geological modeling in data-limited environments, offering a methodological advancement for geological hazard analysis and mineral resource exploration.

# Adaptive Battery Scheduling for Renewable-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging

**Author Information**  
Nicoy Morrison<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Milwaukee School of Engineering  



## Abstract

Efficiently integrating renewable energy sources with electric vehicle (EV) charging stations requires intelligent energy management. This paper presents a deep reinforcement learning strategy for adaptive scheduling of battery storage in conjunction with renewable energy input. The methodology optimizes energy distribution to EV chargers, minimizing grid dependence and reducing charging costs. Results highlight the potential for significant cost savings and improved utilization of renewable energy in EV charging scenarios.

# A Generative AI Framework for MRT Station Crowd Dynamics and Retail Inventory Management

**Author Information**  
Wei-Zhi Wang<sup>1</sup>, Yi-Chung Chen<sup>1</sup>, Yi-Wun Lin<sup>2</sup>


<sup>1</sup> National Chung Hsing University  

<sup>2</sup> Newscan Co., Ltd.  



## Abstract

Retailers near Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) stations struggle with dynamic replenishment because traditional models fail to capture boundary distributions during extreme events, such as heavy rain or large-scale attractions. Furthermore, an integrated framework translating urban flow into retail demand remains absent. This study proposes a two-stage generative framework combining Conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (CGAN) and Diffusion Models: the first stage utilizes CGAN with spatio-temporal vectors to generate scenario-specific flow data, while the second stage employs Diffusion Models to translate these flows into precise commodity demand. Validation using Taipei Metro data reveals: (1) CGAN significantly outperforms traditional supervised and generative baselines in MAE and MAPE, exhibiting superior numerical precision and stability; (2) the Diffusion model accurately captures non-linear consumption shifts such as "stay-at-home" patterns during rain or beverage surges during events for five major product categories. By bridging urban sensing and retail operations through an interactive platform, this research offers a high-value solution for smart city resilience and precision retail management.



Keywords: Spatio-Temporal Passenger Flow Prediction,  Smart Retail,  Product Demand Modeling,  Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs),  Conditional Diffusion Models (CDM)

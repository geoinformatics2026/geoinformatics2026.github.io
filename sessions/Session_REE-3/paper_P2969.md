## Abstract

Spectral knowledge is fundamental to geospatial analysis, yet its representation is often dispersed across textual descriptions and spectral datasets, limiting semantic integration and structured exploration. This study proposes a spectral knowledge graph framework to formally organize heterogeneous spectral information into a unified semantic structure. A hierarchical ontology schema is designed to integrate materials, spectral features, wavelength regions, physical mechanisms, and contextual entities. Domain range constraints and normalized relation types ensure semantic consistency during graph construction. Structural evaluation demonstrates balanced entity and relation distributions and coherent multi-level connectivity. By emphasizing structured knowledge organization rather than predictive modeling, this work provides an extensible semantic infrastructure for spectral information management and contributes to advancing semantic representation and interoperability in geographic information science.



Keywords: spectral knowledge graph, ontology modeling, geospatial semantics, semantic interoperability, knowledge graph construction

# A Novel Endmember Bundle Extraction Method Based on Modified Adaptive Differential Evolution Algorithm to Address Spectral Variability

**Author Information**

<sup>2</sup> Tsinghua University

## Abstract

The accurate characterization of material composition from hyperspectral imagery is often hindered by spectral variability, a common phenomenon in complex environments. To address this, the endmember bundle extraction method represents a material class using a set of spectra rather than a fixed signature, thereby enhancing model flexibility. However, existing endmember bundle extraction methods typically suffer from high computational cost and limited accuracy when processing high-dimensional data under realistic, variable conditions. To overcome these limitations, we propose an Adaptive Differential Evolution Endmember Bundle Extraction (ADEBE) method. It employs an modified differential evolution algorithm to perform a global optimization for optimal endmember combinations, significantly improving the robustness and accuracy of endmember bundle extraction. Experiments on simulated dataset and urban dataset demonstrate that ADEBE achieves superior overall performance compared to traditional and state-of-the-art methods in terms of spectral fidelity, abundance estimation accuracy, and computational efficiency.



Keywords: Hyperspectral remote sensing, spectral variability, endmember bundle extraction (EBE), differential evolution(DE)

# Poetic Space and Rural Identity: A Study of Poems on Ancient Towns and Villages in Jiangxi Based on Natural Language Processing

**Author Information**  
Weiqiang Ye<sup>1</sup>, Hui Lin<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Jiangxi Normal University  



## Abstract

This study takes Jiangxi’s poems from past dynasties as the research carrier and focuses on the spatio-temporal changes of natural landscapes and humanistic environments in ancient towns and villages of Jiangxi. With the support of natural language processing technology, it explores the internal relationships among geographical images, scenery descriptions, emotional expressions and identity narratives in the texts. Through quantitative analysis, topic extraction and semantic mining of poetic corpora, this research systematically sorts out the evolutionary characteristics of natural ecology, living spaces, folk customs and social structures in different historical periods. It further discusses the emotional projection and identity construction of literati and local residents toward their native spaces in poetic writing. The findings reveal that landscapes, architecture, local products and folk customs in poems not only directly reflect regional natural and humanistic environments, but also serve as important media for local identity, cultural belonging and rural consciousness. Natural language processing provides a quantitative approach for traditional literature and regional culture studies, breaking through the limitations of traditional textual interpretation and supporting the protection of regional culture and the inheritance of rural spaces.

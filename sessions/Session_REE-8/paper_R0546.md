# 'Robot-with-a-Drone' Collaborative System: A Seamless Air-Ground Approach for Fine-Grained Inspection of Cultural Heritage

**Author Information**  
Ling Weijia<sup>1</sup>, Hu Chuli<sup>1</sup>, Wang Ke<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> China University of Geosciences  



## Abstract

Fine-grained inspection of cultural heritage is essential for preventive conservation, yet traditional methods face the dilemma of "inaccessible heights and incomplete ground views." This study proposes a novel tripartite system integrating a quadruped robot, robotic arm, and UAV, with the UAV mounted as a detachable end-effector on the arm for seamless air-ground transition. The system autonomously switches among three modes: 1) robotic arm proximity sensing for millimeter-scale inspection; 2) UAV deployment for aerial mapping of blind spots; and 3) collaborative verification forming an "aerial discovery-ground confirmation" closed loop. Key breakthroughs include dynamic mode switching, multi-source data spatiotemporal registration, and a lightweight deterioration recognition model. Field tests achieved comprehensive coverage of areas previously inaccessible by traditional means, attaining 0.3 mm crack detection accuracy, air-ground data registration errors below 2 cm, and data completeness exceeding 95%. The "robot-with-a-drone" paradigm offers a novel solution for high-precision, blind-spot-free heritage inspection.

# Autonomous GIS: Progress and Challenges

**Author Information**  
Zhenlong Li<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Penn State University  



## Abstract

The rise of generative AI and large language models is driving the emergence and continued development of Autonomous GIS, a new paradigm in which systems can propose hypotheses, collect relevant data, generate geoprocessing workflows, organize resources, execute spatial operations, verify results, and improve through learning from prior operational experience in geographic research. In this talk, I will review recent research and development in Autonomous GIS across academia and industry. I will discuss how Autonomous GIS may reshape geospatial problem solving, research, and practice, why it matters for the future of GIScience research and education, and what challenges continue to limit its broader development and adoption.



Keywords: Autonomous GIS,  Agentic GIS,  Autonomous Spatial Analysis,  GIScience,  GIS Agent

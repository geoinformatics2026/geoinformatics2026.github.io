# Understanding the focused place of travel-related social media texts: A multi-hierarchical cognitive framework for resolving complex place descriptions

**Author Information**  
Xiawei Chen<sup>1</sup>, Yi Long<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> School of Geography, NanJing Normal University  



## Abstract

Social media texts constitute a vital data source for spatial cognition, yet accurately identifying the specific focused place within content-rich posts remains challenging for downstream geolocation applications. Existing geoparsing methods predominantly extract isolated place mentions, struggling to distinguish a user's true focused place description (FPD) from contextual referenced place descriptions (RPDs). To overcome this limitation in high-density travel narratives, we propose a novel multi-hierarchical cognitive framework. This study operationalizes the theoretical framework using a four-step Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI) pipeline driven by Large Language Models (LLMs). The proposed LLM-driven pipeline first exhaustively extracts and categorizes diverse spatial references into place names, relative locations, and route-based descriptions. Next, it reconstructs context-aware semantic relationships—both hierarchical and parallel—and applies rules of narrative salience and hierarchical exclusion to explicitly isolate the FPD. Ultimately, the framework generates a structured, multi- hierarchical cognitive graph that maps the spatial constraints from the peripheral RPDs to the central FPD. Empirical evaluations were conducted using a real-world dataset collected from the RedNote targeting the Zhongshan Mountain National Park. The results demonstrate that our proposed GeoAI framework effectively resolves complex spatial semantics, significantly outperforming traditional sequence labeling models and vanilla LLM prompting baselines. By effectively simulating human spatial reasoning, this study contributes a structured spatial cognitive perspective to geoparsing and provides highly actionable spatial constraints for downstream applications, such as precise geo-localization, fine-grained trajectory mining, and intelligent POI recommendation. 



Keywords: geospatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI),  geospatial cognition,  focused place identification,  large language models(LLMs),  place descriptions,  social media text

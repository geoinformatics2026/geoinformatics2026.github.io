# Delineation of dual-carbon spatial management units in Hunan Province by coupling carbon budget zoning and major functional zones

**Author Information**  
Huihui Feng<sup>1</sup>, Shu Wang<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Central South University  



## Abstract

“Promoting green and low-carbon development according to local conditions” is crucial for China’s ecological civilization construction and “dual carbon” goals. This study analyzed the spatial differentiation of carbon budget across 122 districts/counties in Hunan Province using economic contributive and ecological support coefficients, and proposed a dual-carbon spatial management unit framework by coupling carbon budget zoning with major function-oriented zones (MFOZs). The results showed that: ①In 2022, Hunan’s carbon emissions and absorption reached 32113.51 × 10⁴ t and 45179.35 × 10⁴ t respectively, with high-emission areas expanding and carbon absorption areas remaining stable. ②There were 82 Low energy carbon sink zones, 23 High energy carbon emission zones and 15 Low energy carbon emission zones. Low energy carbon sink zones (67.21%) were mainly located in the hilly forest area around Hunan Province, while the number of High energy carbon emission zones (18.85%) and Low energy carbon emission zones (12.30%) were mainly located in in the economically developed central districts and counties such as Changsha, Zhuzhou, Xiangtan and Hengyang. ③Coupled with the results of the “carbon budget zoning - major function-oriented zones”, the districts and counties of Hunan Province divided into 11 types. Among them, the “Low energy carbon sink - National key ecological function zone” was the most important type (38 districts and counties, a total of 85212 km2). It was mainly located in Zhangjiajie City, Huaihua City, Xiangxi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture, Yongzhou City, Chenzhou City and other western and southern districts with high vegetation coverage. While “Low energy carbon emission - Provincial urbanized zone” (1 district and county, 137 km2) had the smallest unit type, mainly located in Shuangqing District of Shaoyang City. The results provide spatial support for differentiated green low-carbon development and dual carbon goals.



Keywords: carbon budget,  major function-oriented zones,  spatiotemporal differentiation,  dual-carbon spatial management units

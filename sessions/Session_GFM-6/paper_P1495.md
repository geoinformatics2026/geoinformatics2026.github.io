# Spatio-temporal ontology for intergrated watershed system assessment and modeling

**Author Information**  
Shanzhen Yi<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Huazhong University of Science and Technology  



## Abstract

Watershed is a kind of natural geospatial area with complex process and interaction among hydrology and climate, human activity and ecologic environment. Integrated watershed assessment modeling and management are necessary for the watershed collaboration and sustainable development under global change. Geospatial information technologies provide potential approach for the integrated modeling and management problem. However, multidisciplinary, scales, multisource heterogeneous data are potential challenge for the information sharing and integration problem. This paper has proposed a spatio-temporal ontology framework for the integration of watershed system assessment and modeling. Form the view of system science, the sub-systems of natural phenomena and human activity are represented as integrative ontologies. From the view of discrete and continuous property in spatio-temporal dimension, the elements of the sub-systems are represented as faceted ontologies, including object, field, process, state, event and property. An integrated watershed modeling framework based on the spatio-temporal ontology is given. The proposed framework will provide a holism methodology for integrated assessment, modeling and management for complex river basin.

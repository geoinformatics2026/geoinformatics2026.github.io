## Abstract

This study examines the optimal commercial layout of Hulatang (hot and sour soup) shops in Zhengzhou, a representative local specialty dish crucial for urban cultural identity. We propose a Cooperative-Competitive Maximum Coverage Location Problem (CC-MCLP) model to optimize store locations by considering market coverage, brand cooperation, competitive costs, and operational expenses. To address this complex problem, we propose an innovative Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) approach utilizing a Graph Attention Encoder- Transformer Decoder architecture. Our comparative experiments demonstrate that the DRL method's solutions are nearly identical to those of the exact solver, Gurobi, regarding objective function values and market coverage. Crucially, the DRL approach achieves this with computational efficiency that's two orders of magnitude faster than Gurobi. Compared to traditional heuristic algorithms, such as the Genetic Algorithm (GA), our method offers superior solution quality and speed. Beyond Hulatang in Zhengzhou, our framework applies to urban commercial site- selection problems shaped by local cooperation and competition; illustrative applications include convenience stores, coffee chains, fast-food and bakery chains, supermarkets, and lifestyle retailers.

# Cognitive Map Generation Based on Built Environment Networks

**Author Information**  
Qiumeng Li<sup>1</sup>, Xinyue Liu<sup>2</sup>, Chunhou Ji<sup>2</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Hong Kong University of Science and Technology（Guangzhou）  

<sup>2</sup> The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)  



## Abstract

Cognitive maps refer to the internal mental representations of physical environments and spatial relationships formed by humans or animals. While the spatial representation of cognitive maps and their influence on behavioral decision-making have been extensively studied, there remains a lack of computational models capable of representing cognitive maps in complex urban environments, as well as an insufficient understanding of how built environment features shape their formation. To address this gap, this study introduces a computational neuroscience-inspired cognitive map representation framework and conducts systematic research on the representation and generative mechanisms of group-level cognitive maps, supported by large-scale human mobility data. Specifically, this study investigates how cognitive maps can be generated from built environment feature networks and explores the feasibility of predicting cognitive maps in the absence of observed mobility trajectories. This research will advance the mechanistic understanding of how the built environment influences travel behavior from a cognitive perspective and establish a new computational and predictive paradigm for cognitive map research in urban space. Ultimately, it will provide theoretical and methodological support for human-centered spatial cognition modeling and environment optimization.

# A Novel Identification Model of Urban Traffic Crash-Prone Location from Geographical Perspective: BPNN-CA Model

**Author Information**  
Chenyu Wang<sup>1</sup>, Chun Chen<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Chongqing Jiaotong University  



## Abstract

Objective: Traffic crashes are a major problem worldwide. The World Health Organization reported that road traffic crashes caused approximately 1.19 million deaths in 2021. To improve road traffic safety, accurately and effectively identifying Crash-Prone Locations (CPLs) is essential, but traditional models fail to consider crash spatial correlation. This study proposes a novel method for quickly identifying urban road CPLs to address this limitation. Method: This study proposes a combined Back Propagation Neural Network (BPNN)-Cellular Automata (CA) method. The model is validated using 1160 crash data (2010-2021, Yuzhong District, Chongqing). The Equivalent Crash Predictive Accuracy Index (ECPAI) of this method is compared with that of safety performance function, crash frequency, kernel density estimation, and hot spot analysis. Results: The prediction performance of the BPNN-CA model was evaluated using multiple error metrics, yielding a R2 of 0.8106, Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 0.7571, Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) of 1.2351, and kappa coefficient of 0.7607. The model demonstrated efficient predictive capability. Furthermore, the model successfully identified 9 CPLs. The Equivalent Crash Predictive Accuracy Index (ECPAI) was adopted for comparative analysis, with results of 2.2076, 2.5427, 1.6402, 1.1156, and 3.8074 for the safety performance function, crash frequency method, kernel density estimation, hot spot analysis, and the proposed BPNN-CA model, respectively. These results confirm that the BPNN-CA model is the most effective method for CPL identification. Conclusions: This study proposes a novel framework that integrates BPNN and CA to identify urban road CPLs. The framework integrates the parallel computing capability of CA and the robust predictive power of BPNN, while also accounting for the influence of surrounding grids on the central grid. The proposed method demonstrates superior accuracy and reliability compared to existing models, offering an effective tool for enhancing road safety planning.



Keywords: traffic safety,  crash-prone locations,  backpropagation neural network,  cellular automata,  crash prediction

# Spatiotemporal Coupling of Land Use Carbon Emissions and Ecosystem Service Value in Typical Oasis Cities in Arid Regions: A Case Study of Urumqi, Xinjiang

**Author Information**  
Bumairiyemu Maimaiti<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Xinjiang Agricultural University  



## Abstract

The rapid urbanization of oasis cities in arid zones has profoundly altered their land-use structures, leading to substantial urban expansion and the encroachment upon vast ecological lands. This transformation significantly impacts CE and ESV. This study using locally calibrated CE and ESV coefficients, calculated total carbon sinks, carbon sources, annual net CE, and total ESV for 5 time period. To further investigate the spatiotemporal relationships between CE and ESV, a coupled coordination model was employed, based on which five ecological functional zones were proposed. Key findings reveal that: (1) Grassland and unused land accounting for over 80% across all study periods. Grassland and cultivated land experienced the largest net area loss, while urban land showed the most significant gain. (2) 1990-2020, CE increased cumulatively by 3.57 thousand tons, with annual growth rate of 8.03%. CEI shifted towards higher-value grids, with urban land being the primary contributor to CE, driving sharp increases in both total CE and CEI. (3) ESV declined steadily by a total of 557.40 million¥, with generally low ESVI values. Grids initially characterized by high ESVI transitioned towards lower values. (4) A strong negative correlation exists between the rising CE and declining ESV, and the degree of coupling coordination showed a consistent downward trend annually. This trend is particularly pronounced within urban built-up areas, highlighting the need for immediate action. (5) Moran's I index overall displayed an upward trend, suggesting an increasingly clustered spatial distribution pattern. Geographical detector analysis indicates that the interaction between population and local fiscal revenue is relatively strong, implying that these two factors are the dominant drivers affecting the spatial correlation between CE and ESV. Based on this, we divides Urumqi into five distinct ecological zones and proposes tailored recommendations for each zone to mitigate CE, enhance ESV, and facilitate rational urban land planning.



Keywords: carbon emissions,  ecosystem service values,  spatio-temporal coupling,  geodetector

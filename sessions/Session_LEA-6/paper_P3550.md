# Environmental and Social Management of Rural Road Maintenance Projects in Yemen ---A Case Study of Al karishah-Al-Turbah Road in Taiz Governorate

**Author Information**  
Ahmed Sana Abdulrkeeb Mohammed<sup>1</sup>, Qiuyi Zhang<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Fujian University of Technology  



## Abstract

 Transportation infrastructure plays a pivotal role in enhancing economic and social development, particularly in rural areas of developing countries where residents heavily rely on roads to access markets and essential services. This paper presents a comprehensive assessment of environmental, social, health, and occupational safety aspects of the Al karishah-Al-Turbah road maintenance project in Taiz Governorate, Yemen. The study employed a case study approach to evaluate the project's Environmental and Social Management Plan (ESMP) implementation, analyzing Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSSE) inspection reports and technical documentation. The results demonstrate that systematic application of environmental and social management procedures significantly contributed to reducing environmental impacts and improving working conditions. The project enhanced community access to essential services and promoted local economic activities, providing valuable insights for sustainable rural infrastructure development in conflict-affected regions.

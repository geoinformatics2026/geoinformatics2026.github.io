# Decline or prosperity ? Decoding the spatio-temporal evolution and types of China's rural areal system vitality

**Author Information**  
Dabao Zhou<sup>1</sup>, Dan Gao<sup>1</sup>, Chaoyang Fang<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> School of Geography and Environment, Jiangxi Normal University  



## Abstract

The rural areal system (RAS) serves as a multidimensional focal point for rural revitalization in China's new era. Declining rural vitality presents a pervasive challenge faced by many countries globally. However, the spatio-temporal dynamics of vitality within the RAS remain unclear, necessitating an understanding of these dynamics and the establishment of a definitive typology relevant to China. To address this, we developed an evaluation framework for RAS vitality based on multi-source geographic big data from 2013 to 2023, grounded in classical mechanics and adhering to the “element-direction-vitality” logic. Utilizing spatial autocorrelation analysis, and triangle model, we quantitatively analyze the dynamic evolution and classification of vitality across 1867 county-level units in China. Our findings indicate an upward trend in the vitality of rural human settlement, land, and industry. Notably, areas of high human settlement vitality do not entirely coincide with economically developed regions, while industrial and land vitality exhibit significant discrepancies on either side of the Hu Line. The endogenous expansion force associated with human settlement, the exogenous development force of land, and the endogenous support force of industry demonstrate minimal changes. Conversely, the exogenous attraction force of human settlement, the endogenous carrying capacity of land, and the exogenous driving force of industry have significantly increased. The RAS vitality is significantly different between the east and the west. We classify the vitality of the RAS into seven types, with human settlement vitality being the most predominant, primarily concentrated in Northeast, Northwest, and Southeast China. Finally, we propose tailored policy recommendations based on the strengths and weaknesses identified in different types and regions. This study offers valuable insights for understanding the spatio-temporal evolution of vitality in China's RAS and for advancing the implementation of the rural revitalization strategy.

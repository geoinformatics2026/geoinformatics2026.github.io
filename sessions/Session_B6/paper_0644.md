## Abstract

Monitoring leaf biochemical traits such as chlorophyll (chl) and carotenoid (car) content is essential for assessing plant health and detecting early physiological stress. Traditional pigment determination methods are destructive and limited in spatial coverage, restricting their application at stand and landscape scales.

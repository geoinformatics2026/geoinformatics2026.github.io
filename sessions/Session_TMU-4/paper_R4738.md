# Efficient detection method for road cracks driven by data and knowledge in complex dynamic environments

**Author Information**  
Ping Wang<sup>1</sup>, Jun Zhu<sup>2</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Zhengzhou University of Light Industry  

<sup>2</sup> Southwest Jiaotong University  



## Abstract

Addressing the issues of weak crack detection capability and difficulty in obtaining key information due to the complex visual information and dynamic uncertainty of road crack video inspection environments, this paper intends to conduct in-depth research on efficient road crack detection methods driven by data and knowledge in complex dynamic environments. The main contents include: (1) an efficient extraction algorithm for video key frames constrained by road crack ridge features, enabling efficient perception of crack frames in road inspection scenarios; (2) a precise semantic segmentation model for road cracks in video images that aggregates global and local features, achieving accurate detection of road cracks in complex dynamic environments; (3) a geometric information calculation method for road cracks in video images constrained by skeleton features, enabling high-precision geometric calculation of road cracks in video images. The results show that compared with existing classic and state-of-the-art video image crack detection methods, our method significantly improves Dice and MIOU, significantly enhancing the efficiency of road crack inspection, effectively increasing the detection speed by 5-7 times, and meeting millimeter-level maximum width detection requirements.

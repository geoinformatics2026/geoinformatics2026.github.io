# A Multi-Stage Vertiport Location Problem for eVTOL Rescue under Flood Disasters: A Case Study of Zhengzhou

**Author Information**  
Changchang Dong<sup>1</sup>, Shiyan Zhai<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Henan University  



## Abstract

Frequent flood disasters often lead to the disruption of urban road networks, severely threatening the safety of affected populations. Electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft (eVTOL) offer a new approach for precise rescue operations due to their technology that is not constrained by ground transportation. The location selection of vertiports is essential for rescue efficiency.This study focuses on the location selection problem of vertiports in flood disasters. Using Zhengzhou City as the study area, and establishes an integer programming model with the objective of maximizing the coverage rate of rescue demands to identify the optimal locations of the vertiport sites. This study differs from existing ones in that it considers the changing demands across different disaster stages.Remote sensing image data is used to classify risk levels, and combined with the proportion of the elderly population, the rescue demand for different disaster stages is calculated. At the same time, the potential sites are screened in both 2D and 3D spaces, and a set of candidate facilities is obtained. Finally, the location of the vertiport is determined based on the maximum coverage location problem (MCLP). The impact of demand, the number of facilities, and the evolution of warning levels on the model solution is also discussed. Although this study uses Zhengzhou as a research object, the proposed methodology is generalized and applicable to any other city or region at high risk of flooding, providing a scientific basis and decision-making reference for the preliminary planning of urban emergency rescue networks.

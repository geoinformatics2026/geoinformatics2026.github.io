# Geospatial Location Optimization of Convenience Stores in Beijing Using the Maximal Covering Location Problem

**Author Information**  
Haozheng Tian<sup>1</sup>, Shaohua Wang<sup>1</sup>, Haojian Liang<sup>1</sup>, Jingyi Zhou<sup>1</sup>


<sup>1</sup> Aerospace Information Research Institute  



## Abstract

Abstract With the rapid expansion of chain convenience stores in urban areas and growing demand for convenient livelihood services, scientific spatial layout optimization has become crucial for brands to enhance competitiveness, improve service accessibility, and utilize urban resources efficiently. Taking "Bianlifeng"-a representative brand with algorithm- driven innovation-as the research subject, this paper analyzes its spatial distribution and influencing factors in Beijing, addressing problems of uneven layout, inadequate coverage, and unreasonable site selection. The Maximal Covering Location Problem (MCLP) model is applied, combining genetic algorithms (GA) and Gurobi optimizer for comparative optimization. Results show Gurobi significantly outperforms GA in both solution efficiency and coverage performance: it achieves higher coverage rate and wider scope, while GA has advantages in faster computing time, together providing comprehensive optimization references. This study provides a new scientific method for Bianlifeng's location optimization, enhances service layout rationality, and strengthens its market competitiveness, while offering insights for other brands and promoting the urban convenience store industry's high-quality development.



Keywords: Convenience Stores,  Spatial Optimization,  MCLP

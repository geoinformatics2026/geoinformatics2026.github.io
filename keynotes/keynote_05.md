## Speaker Introduction

Maria Gonzalez is Full Professor of GIScience and head of the GeoLab at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya in Barcelona. Her work examines the theoretical underpinnings of participatory mapping, data quality in volunteered geographic information, and the ethics of citizen-generated spatial data. Prof. Gonzalez is a founding editor of the International Journal of Geographic Information and a past president of the European Association of Geographic Information Laboratories. She received her doctoral degree from the University of Zurich and is known for her interdisciplinary approach bridging humanistic geography with computational methods.

## Speaker Introduction

Sarah Okonkwo leads the Spatial Development Unit at the World Bank, where she oversees a global portfolio of earth observation and GIS projects spanning poverty mapping, infrastructure planning, and environmental monitoring across more than 40 countries. Dr. Okonkwo holds a PhD in Geography from Clark University and graduate degrees in development economics from LSE. Before joining the World Bank, she was a postdoctoral fellow at Columbia University's Earth Institute. Her current work focuses on harmonising national geospatial data infrastructures to accelerate Sustainable Development Goal monitoring, with a particular emphasis on data equity and institutional capacity building in least-developed countries.

## About the talk

This talk introduces Spatio-Temporal Foundation Models (STFMs) — an emerging paradigm that integrates spatial topology with temporal evolution to build unified cross-domain representations for urban sensing, simulation, and spatial decision-making — and presents their industrial practice in Shanghai's Zhangjiang AI Town. At the core is WAYZ's PhyGital Foundation Model, the first multimodal STFM trained on the town's 20,000+ data classes, from geography and enterprises to traffic, power, events, urban operations, and satellite imagery, enabling it to model, predict, memorize, and learn urban space. On top, digital agents deliver forecasts and decisions, while physical agents including drones, vehicles, and robots turn decisions into action — each action and sensing stream writing back into spatiotemporal memory, closing a self-evolving STFM loop. This offers a replicable roadmap for city-scale spatial agents. Language models predict the next token; STFMs predict the city's next state.

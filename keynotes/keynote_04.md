## Speaker Introduction

Kenji Nakamura is Professor of Urban Informatics at the University of Tokyo's Institute of Industrial Science. His research spans agent-based simulation, spatiotemporal big data analytics, and human mobility modelling, with applications in transit planning and public health preparedness. He played a key role in Japan's national COVID-19 mobility analysis task force and has collaborated extensively with city governments in Tokyo, Osaka, and Singapore. Prof. Nakamura holds a PhD from Kyoto University and has received the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science Prize. He is currently leading a cross-national project on real-time pandemic early-warning systems using mobile phone data.
